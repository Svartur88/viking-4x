## What to judge them on

Not "is it pretty". Three questions:
1. Do the three read as **one game**? If the terrain and the longhouse look like different products, the style bible is too loose.
2. Does the longhouse still look like **the same building** at stage 3 as at stage 1?
3. Does the daughter look like **those two people's child**?

Hawk's yes/no on these is the gate before the ~47 hours of asset work starts (art-pipeline.md).
