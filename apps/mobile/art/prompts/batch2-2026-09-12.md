## Verdict, honestly

- **Markers: excellent.** Genuinely straight down, readable small, and the three hall stages grow. Exactly what DEC-012 asked for.
- **Buildings: excellent.** Consistent with the Longhouse in angle, palette and turf base.
- **Terrain: usable, but off-style.** It came back photographic rather than painted, for the second time — the model reads "top-down ground texture" as aerial photography however the prompt is worded. It is in the game because it beats flat colour close up, but it does not match the painted buildings, and the style bible says painted wins. Worth a third attempt with a much heavier painterly push (explicit "gouache", "visible brush strokes", "not a photograph", maybe a different model), or accept the split. Hawk's call.

Note on the terrain in play: ground detail is drawn only in the Near band. Zoomed out, a photographic material squashed into twelve pixels a tile reads as either a repeating grid or as static, so Mid uses flat token colours instead. Tried both the ordered-window and hashed-window variants at Mid; both looked worse than plain colour.
